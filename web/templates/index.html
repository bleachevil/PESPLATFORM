How a season works

  1. The host sets league name, max players, and number of weeks. The desk builds first half, winter, second half, and summer.
  2. Managers ask to join. A host or officer has to approve before a club is in the league.
  3. Available, Signed, and In the sea tell you who you can pick.
  4. After a first squad, sign only in the winter/summer sea windows. New clubs can still build that first squad.
  5. You play the fixture in eFootball. This site keeps the books.
